# Privacy Policy for X Bot Detector

Last updated: 8 May 2026

X Bot Detector is a Chrome extension that scores X (formerly Twitter) profiles and reply threads for bot likelihood. This privacy policy describes what data the extension handles and how.

## What the extension reads

The extension reads publicly visible information from X profile and thread pages that you actively view, including:

- Account username, display name, and bio text
- Follower count, following count, tweet count, listed count
- Account creation date (account age in days)
- Verified status
- Default avatar status
- Reply usernames and verified status (on thread pages)

The extension only reads this information from x.com and twitter.com pages you actively view. It does not access other tabs, your browsing history, your personal information, your messages, or any non-X data.
